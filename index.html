Your People. Our HR Expertise.

HR Deck Studio is a Bengaluru-based HR consultancy, recruitment and training firm focused on helping organizations build stronger teams and practical HR processes.

We support startups, SMEs and growing organizations with recruitment, HR operations, employee documentation, onboarding, policies, performance management and HR consulting.

We also provide practical HR training for freshers and working professionals who want to develop job-ready HR skills.
